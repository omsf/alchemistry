Test your wits in our challenge of 54 compounds with measured potency for the Dengue-2 NS2B-NS3 protease. This challenge was enabled in collaboration with ASAP Discovery, OpenADMET and OpenBind.

We invite the alchemical free energy calculation community to compete in this challenge. Does your method combined with your system preparation skills have what it takes to top the leaderboard? Additionally, we also welcome non-alchemical methods such as pure machine learning approaches.

- Join the challenge on our [HuggingFace Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/openadmet/alchemistry-2027-challenge){: .intro-link}
- Join our [Discord channel](https://discord.gg/fC3XGwEJW){: .intro-link}
- Check out the [tutorial]({{ '/blind_challenges/2027/tutorial/' | relative_url }}){: .intro-link}

We have already populated the leaderboard with some first submissions. We have included a low-effort and high-effort benchmark with an RBFE method to show that achieving ranking power is possible. We also show that the potency of this compound series is partially related to molecular weight - any method that beats this baseline is showing real promise for drug discovery.
