# Description
The **2010 Workshop on Free Energy Methods in Drug Design** was the inaugural workshop in this series. It was held May 17-19, 2010, in the Kendall Lecture Hall at Vertex Pharmaceuticals, 675 West Kendall Street, Cambridge, Massachusetts. Although free energy methods have long promised to revolutionize drug discovery, and advances in computational power, efficient algorithms, and force field development suggested that this promise could soon be realized, a "last mile" problem remained in bringing these techniques to the point where they could drive drug discovery in both pharma and academia. The workshop brought together experts from pharma, supporting industries, and academia to identify the drug discovery challenges where free energy methods might have the most impact, to enumerate the challenges remaining before these methods could find widespread use in ligand and drug design, and to organize working groups to tackle those challenges after the meeting. The program combined 25-minute talks, 10-minute talks, and a poster session, with ample time for discussion; presentations of problems, limitations, and failures were explicitly encouraged.
