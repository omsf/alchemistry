# Description
The **2012 Workshop on Free Energy Methods in Drug Design** was held May 21-23, 2012, hosted by Vertex Pharmaceuticals at 675 Kendall St in Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA. The workshop focused on free energy techniques: the latest innovations and use cases, force field parameterization as it touches these techniques, and sampling practices. It brought together experts from pharma, supporting industries, and academia in an intense and focused meeting to assess where these methods stood, identify the challenges that had to be tackled to bring them to bear on real-world drug discovery applications, and identify particular problems in drug discovery where they could have an impact. Talks addressed four main themes: (1) use cases for free energy techniques in real-world problems where they can or have had an impact; (2) how pharma can benefit today or in the immediate future; (3) parameterization and how the latest innovations affect free energies; and (4) good sampling practices, the challenges facing sampling, and their impact on free energy estimates. Participants were encouraged to go beyond success stories and discuss problems, limitations, and failures, along with ideas for overcoming them.
